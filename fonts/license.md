# Google Fonts provenance and licenses

On 2026-09-21, Andre von Houck confirmed that the project's fonts came
from Google Fonts. All four included font files below use the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (SPDX: OFL-1.1), as recorded in their embedded metadata.
They meet the project's open-source asset policy and retain their OFL
licenses rather than the CC0 dedication for generated UI artwork.

| Family | Files relative to polyworld_art | License notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rubik | `fonts/Rubik-Regular.ttf`, `fonts/Rubik-Bold.ttf` | [Rubik OFL](OFL-Rubik.txt) |
| Overpass Mono | `fonts/OverpassMono-Regular.ttf` | [Overpass Mono OFL](OFL-OverpassMono.txt) |
| IBM Plex Sans | `themes/main/IBMPlexSans-Regular.ttf` | [IBM Plex Sans OFL](../themes/main/OFL-IBMPlexSans.txt) |

Copyright notices embedded in the checked-in fonts:

- Rubik: Copyright 2015 The Rubik Project Authors.
- Overpass Mono: Copyright 2021 The Overpass Project Authors.
- IBM Plex Sans: Copyright 2018 IBM Corp. All rights reserved.

The IBM Plex upstream notice additionally records Copyright 2017 IBM
Corp. and the Reserved Font Name "Plex". Both that notice and the
checked-in font's embedded notice are retained.

The complete license texts were checked against the Google Fonts
repository on 2026-09-21:

- [Rubik](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/rubik/OFL.txt).
- [Overpass Mono](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/overpassmono/OFL.txt).
- [IBM Plex Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/ibmplexsans/OFL.txt).

Preserve the applicable copyright notices and license when distributing
the fonts. This provenance notice does not change their license terms.
