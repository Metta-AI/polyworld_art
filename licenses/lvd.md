# Light vs Dark generated asset licenses

LvD uses the approved CharGen roster in `characters/chargen/lvd.json`.
Its body, clothing, equipment, face parts and rendered portraits are
project-generated assets under the [root CC0 dedication](../LICENSE).
Both players use the same eight roles, with shared faction colors applied
to their skin and UI. The current match uses Peter River and Amethyst from
the eight-color palette in `themes/lvd/factions.md`.
The fire elemental is orange for both players, with no face parts or ears.

Animation clips come from the
[CC0 Quaternius Universal set](../animations/quaternius/universal_standard/README.txt).
The CharGen legacy exceptions are excluded. Former Mini Legion and Demon
King unit models and portraits are no longer loaded or browser-packed.

Missing art remains explicit: peon pickaxe and mining animation, a dedicated
bow animation, and a bomb model and bomb-launching animation. The peon uses
the CC0 interaction clip; archer and bomber use CC0 pistol clips as temporary
ranged animations. The bomber retains the old siege splash attack and uses
the existing procedural fire projectile and burst until a bomb is available.

Unit UI badges now use `themes/lvd/unit-symbols.png`, an original generated
3 by 3 sheet of white role symbols on grayscale cloth, covered by the root
CC0 dedication. Each badge is tinted by the owning team's skin color in
the UI. The former CharGen portraits remain available for review but are
no longer loaded or browser-packed. Building portraits are still used and
retain the licenses of their source models.

Forests and decorative boulders use the shared GotA TreeGen and RockGen
presets. Their geometry is generated by the project; the three textures in
`terrain/treegen/` and `terrain/rockgen/rock-trim-atlas.png` are project
assets under the root CC0 dedication. The former Handpainted Forest tree
models/textures and Toon Enchanted Meadow rock models/textures are no
longer loaded or browser-packed by LvD.

Ground textures use the same CC0 tile and stamp library as GotA: the nine
shared grass, dirt, cobble, gravel, forest, and marsh surfaces, plus
`crypt-rock-1` and `courtyard-stone-1`. These tile/stamp images are covered
by the root CC0 dedication. LvD no longer loads or packs the CC BY grass
scatter model or the imported MIT water normal maps. Grass scatter art is
left out until a CC0 replacement exists; water uses the shared shader with
neutral normals created in memory and no external water image assets.

Buildings now use the nine owner-confirmed CC0 models in
`terrain/lvd_buildings/models/`, shared by both players. Their embedded
textures and newly rendered portraits are covered by the root dedication.
Gold mines combine the new timber entrance with RockGen boulders.
Construction sites and rubble use the same cleared buildings and rocks.
The former village and tower defense models and portraits are no longer
loaded or browser-packed. See `terrain/lvd_buildings/license.md`.

Every tenth mirrored tree pair becomes permanent RockGen rocks, preserving
map symmetry. These rocks block movement and contain no harvestable wood.

All selected model and image artwork is now covered by the CC0 asset
dedication or the CC0 animation license. Rubik and Overpass Mono fonts
remain SIL OFL 1.1, and project source code remains MIT.
