# Call to Adventure asset licenses

CtA uses generated chargen characters and portraits dedicated under the
[root CC0 license](../LICENSE). The approved monster recipes are in
`characters/chargen/cta.json`. Hero presets reuse GotA's Vanguard Knight,
Arcanist, Ranger and Druid Warden. Outfits, weapons and face parts are
existing project-generated assets. No new geometry was created for this port.

Animation clips come from Quaternius Universal Standard, under
[CC0-1.0](../animations/quaternius/universal_standard/README.txt).

The former modular character pack, orc, footman, lich and rock golem models
are no longer loaded or included in CtA's browser asset declaration. Floor
loot uses existing project-generated HUD and ability icons under the root
license. Rubik and Overpass Mono fonts retain their bundled SIL OFL notices.

Terrain now uses the existing generated tile and stamp color/height pairs
under the root CC0 dedication. These are the nine base surfaces in
`terrain/tiles/` and `terrain/stamps/`, plus `crypt-rock-1`, `crypt-rock-2`,
`crypt-stone-1`, `crypt-flagstone-1`, `crypt-lava-1`, `crypt-lava-2` and
`cobble-tan-1` tile pairs. The former Unity CartoonTerrain textures and unused
tree, rock, grass and water models/textures are not selected by CtA.

The current CtA asset selection has no known remaining restricted art from
the 2026-09-22 audit. This notice covers CtA's selected assets, not the entire
shared repository. Imported chargen exceptions and assets used by other games
retain their original notices.
