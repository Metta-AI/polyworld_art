# Spore Bloom textures

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. These grayscale sprites are
uploaded once, then tinted and animated by the GPU particle renderer.
Black texels are masked before blending. Reference and preview images
are stored in `polyworld/tmp/fx/`.

## petal.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: grayscale particle sprite for a Spore Bloom game spell.
Primary request: one tall elegant pointed leaf-shaped magical petal on pure black.
Composition: square texture, centered vertical lancet silhouette; razor tip at top center and narrow pointed base at bottom center. Wide middle, crisp polygonal planes like a translucent botanical crystal, a bright fine center vein, luminous white outer edge, layered medium gray faces. All of the leaf is inside the central 65 percent width with 8 percent margin top and bottom.
Style: hand-painted anime game VFX, simplified bold facets, graceful botanical shape.
Constraints: grayscale only, white and gray on uniform pure black; no stem, no plant, no extra leaves, no sphere, no text, no scene. Single reusable particle texture.
```
