[LICENSE](../../LICENSE). These assets remain free to use, modify and
redistribute, including commercially.

The collection was moved from the water experiment in `Metta-AI/polyworld`
on 2026-09-22, from a checkout based on commit `4345758`. Its texture and
preview dedication was already recorded in both repositories' license files.

- `textures/generated/`: seven original project images made with the
  built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-08. The thicker foam variant edits the
  original foam image. The prompt notes record the process and the generic
  terminology update made during migration.
- `textures/`: eight original procedural textures and previews generated by
  Polyworld's `experiments/cartoon_water/gen_textures.nim`.
- `previews/`: four rendered captures of the experiment's procedural ocean,
  island and shoreline using its project-generated textures.

The experiment references
[Dooge's water material breakdown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8NL9Cc05CYk)
as a technique reference. No images from that video are included here.

All 19 PNGs retain their original bytes. Only paths and accompanying text
were changed. Shader and generator source code remain in Polyworld under
the MIT license. No third-party game assets are included in this collection.
