# Low Poly Grass

- **Asset:** Four low-poly grass clump variants.
- **Format:** Binary glTF 2.0 with geometry and materials.
- **Contents:** Four static meshes with no rig or animations.
- **Node layout:** Every model is an independent top-level scene node.
- **Useful for:** Ground cover, fields, gardens, and scattered vegetation.

## Node chart

```text
Scene
├── grass1 (mesh)
├── grass2 (mesh)
├── grass3 (mesh)
└── grass4 (mesh)
```

## Models available

- `grass1`
- `grass2`
- `grass3`
- `grass4`

## Animations available

- None. All models are static.
