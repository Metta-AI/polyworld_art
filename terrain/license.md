# Low Poly Grass Pack

`low_poly_grass.glb` is a renamed download of
[Low Poly Grass Pack](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-grass-pack-2ffb4d5302c14d038eaf6488b8c7ede2)
by [Anskar](https://sketchfab.com/Anskar), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The source attribution and license were verified on 2026-08-25 and retained
from the previous asset repository. The migration preserves the GLB bytes.

Polyworld's browser asset pipeline repacks the GLB and externalizes its
texture data. Rendering places, scales and shades the grass within generated
terrain. The original artist does not endorse this project.

Keep the creator, title, source link, license link and indication of changes
with redistributed copies. The CC0 default does not apply to this model.
