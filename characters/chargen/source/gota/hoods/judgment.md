# Hood visual review

Final verdict: **PASS** for the requested Crossbowman, Lich, and Warlock hood corrections.

Reviewed the approved roster and individual clothing concepts against the actual front, side, and back hood renders. Also reviewed all three full characters in rest, walk, and crouch poses. The final pass rechecked the comparison sheet and equipped Lich and Warlock models after trim cleanup.

- The front openings remain open below the face. There is no continuous cloth or trim ring following the underside of the jaw.
- Broader lower side panels flare away from the cheeks, fold inward, and terminate in separate points beside the neck. They read as draped cloth rather than a head wrap or narrow dangling helmet tabs.
- The rear shells remain rounded and close to the skull. The lower rear cloth covers the previously exposed scalp in the rest pose.
- The Lich ivory and Warlock gold borders now follow the lower physical hem continuously around the sides and rear. The abrupt side cutoffs are resolved.
- Crossbowman brown cloth, Lich crystal crown, and Warlock horns retain their character identities. The Ranger is visually unchanged in the comparison.

Review iterations rejected the first tight oval opening and then the overly narrow side tabs with high rear scalp exposure. The revised panel geometry passed; the final trim cleanup also passes.

Limitations: these are stylized rigidly attached cloth meshes, not simulated fabric. Pitching the head in walk or crouch can reveal the neck and underside through the open bottom, and front points move with the head. The renders do not constitute validation of every possible animation frame. No remaining blocking hood-shape defect is visible in the inspected views.
