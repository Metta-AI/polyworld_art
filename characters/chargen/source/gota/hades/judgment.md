# Hades visual review

Verdict: **PASS** for the Gota character adaptation on the existing rig.

Reviewed the original two-god concept, all eight generated clothing and
equipment concept sheets, actual modular item front/back captures, and the
assembled model from front, rear, and both sides in rest, walk, and crouch.

The revised shoulders have angular upturned outlines and curved depth.
The black spiked crown, green gems, pointed beard, charcoal armor, skull
buckle, burgundy cape, and long tabard preserve the source identity. The
gold belt edges now remain visible around the waist. The original side
scalp gaps are closed, and the shin guards fit against the boot surfaces.

The cape follows the bent back during crouch rather than rotating upward
into a shelf. The last tabard revision uses a shared black face and gold
perimeter surface; the broad gold breakthrough seen in the prior walk
capture is resolved. The final walk and crouch retain a coherent black
tabard and narrow gold border.

Independent inspection of the exported GLBs confirms zero textures and
each modular item below 5000 triangles; the largest is Hair at 3488. The
final assembled authoring report is 19316 triangles. The existing rig,
head, eyes, brows, mouth, and nose are reused.

This is a simplified solid-color adaptation. Small ornament and the
concept's light bloom are not reproduced literally. Cloth is skinned
rather than simulated; deep crouch compresses and overlaps skirt layers.
The inspected stills do not validate every animation frame. No remaining
blocking defect is visible in the reviewed captures.
