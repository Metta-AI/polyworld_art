# Gota appearance review

The ten presets use existing eye modules and natural human skin colors. The Lich remains a pale human, Death Knight a dark-skinned human, and Demon Hunter keeps concealed eyes under the blindfold.

Curved helmet shells, hoods, horns, boots and cuffs now use smooth normals
with sharp garment edges. The Ranger has a smaller rounded hood with a
separate braids-only hair piece. Her hood conceals her ears, and removing it
restores them. The Crossbowman uses a trimmed hood fringe. The Lich has a
closer-fitting hood and no hair.

Verified in actual runtime front/back, walking and crouching renders. All ten remain below 20,000 triangles. Shared library verification and Chargen tests pass. Costume design reviews in individual folders predate these appearance changes.
