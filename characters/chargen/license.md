# Chargen asset sources

The active CharGen library uses project-generated character parts and the
Quaternius Universal Standard animations. Project-generated assets are covered
by the repository's [root license](../../LICENSE). The animations retain their
[Quaternius license and attribution](../../animations/quaternius/universal_standard/README.txt).

The 53 legacy Unity animation and pose exports, the Layer Lab Original2 eyes,
and the Neutral, Happy and Angry eye variants with unresolved provenance were
removed from this directory on 2026-09-22. Their actions, eye meshes, textures
and old body comparison image were also removed from the authoring Blender
files. The removal is recorded in `source/clean_sources.json`.

Historical versions of those removed assets retain their original licensing
restrictions. This cleanup does not relicense them. The separate legacy
character kit is not a CharGen dependency.
